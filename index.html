GOOD QUESTIONS

A few things
before we start.

What should I bring?+

Bring your notes, course materials, a calculator, and specific questions. Practice problems are welcome; we won’t complete graded work for you.

What if I need to cancel?+

Cancel through your private booking link at least 24 hours before your session for a full refund. Later cancellations and no-shows owe no more than the agreed maximum, subject to tutor-approved exceptions and applicable law.

Will my group price go up if someone cancels?+

Never above your accepted maximum. After the session, checked-in attendance determines the pricing tier. Attendees pay the lower of that tier and their individual maximum. Late cancellations and no-shows do not receive an attendance discount.

Where do sessions take place?+

Choose in-person in Oxford or online when available. The location and online meeting room are demo placeholders until configured by the tutor.

Is this a university service?+

No. This is independent tutoring and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Mississippi.
