Booking terms

ACCY Tutoring is an independent tutoring service, not affiliated with or endorsed by the University of Mississippi. Sessions last 60 minutes, start and end at the scheduled time, and are offered in the format shown at booking. Late arrivals do not extend the session.

New bookings require at least 12 hours’ notice and can be made up to 30 days ahead. Group registration closes 2 hours before start, while the advance-notice rule also applies. Tutors may override the advance-notice requirement. Group course, topic, time, location, and format are set by the tutor.

Checkout reserves a spot for up to ten minutes. Your booking is confirmed only after test checkout succeeds. Location, tutor biography, and online meeting details remain editable demo placeholders. The business domain is accytutor.com; this prototype is a static presentation demo.

Your group price is protected

You expressly accept a maximum price before checkout. In the prototype, that maximum is collected in simulation. It never increases because others cancel or fail to attend. After the session ends and attendance is recorded, attending students pay the lower of the actual-attendance tier or their personal maximum. Any difference is a simulated partial refund.

Another attendee with a higher maximum can pay a different amount. Existing sessions retain the schedule displayed when they were created.

Late cancellations and no-shows do not count as physically present and do not receive attendance discounts; their expressly agreed amount remains due, subject to tutor exceptions and applicable law. Early cancellations are fully refunded and excluded. Groups may proceed with one attendee. Group pricing is available for Introductory Accounting and Intermediate I and II. Existing groups keep the price tiers saved when the tutor created them.

Cancellation & refunds

Cancel through your private booking link through 24 hours before the scheduled start for a full refund of any collected amount. Your exact deadline is saved with your booking. Within that window, cancellations and no-shows owe no more than the accepted maximum. There is no additional penalty.

If the tutor cancels, you receive a full refund; rescheduling is voluntary and must be arranged with you. The tutor can approve exceptions, including when applicable law requires them. Contact the tutor to discuss an exception; online self-cancellation ends when the session begins. All refunds in this prototype are simulated ledger entries.

Privacy notice

We collect your name, email, course, topic, questions, optional professor and notes, booking and attendance status, agreed maximum, and policy acceptance record to run tutoring sessions. Avoid including sensitive personal information in your notes. No student account or card details are collected in this prototype.

This presentation uses fictional sample records. Any details you enter stay in your browser session and are not submitted to the tutor. Closing the tab or resetting the demo clears your changes. The dashboard is a public demonstration, not an authenticated account. No payment, calendar, or email provider is connected.

Before launch, the tutor must establish a retention and deletion policy and identify any live processors. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact hello@example.com (editable demo contact; replace before use).

Academic integrity

Tutoring explains concepts and works through practice. The tutor will not complete graded assignments or exams, impersonate you, or help bypass course rules. You remain responsible for your own submitted work and your course’s collaboration policy.

The tutor may decline future online bookings for misconduct. Contact the tutor to discuss the reason or request a review. Restrictions do not automatically cancel or remove rights relating to existing bookings.
